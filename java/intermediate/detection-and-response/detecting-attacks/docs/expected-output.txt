TWO. Two attacks, worked out.
  THE INPUT (attack A, every failure from 203.0.113.50):
    79 failures on 40 accounts, 10:00:00 to 10:21:15
  PER ACCOUNT (what the brute-force rule counts):
    most failures, 1 account, 5 minutes  2
    2 is under 10  <- BRUTE FORCE STAYS QUIET
  PER ADDRESS (what the spraying rule counts):
    u101 10:00:00, u102 10:00:25, ... u108 10:02:55
    different accounts in 20 minutes  8 at 10:02:55
    8 reaches 8  <- SPRAYING FIRES
  THE INPUT (attack C, u088's two successful logins):
    15:00:00  Madrid    10.3.0.89
    15:25:00  Sydney    192.0.2.88
  THE SPEED (worked out):
    distance  17685 km, Madrid to Sydney
    time      25 minutes, 15:00 to 15:25
    speed     17685 x 60 / 25 = 42444 km/h
    42444 is over 900  <- IMPOSSIBLE TRAVEL FIRES

    distance  5837 km, Paris to New York
    time      540 minutes, 08:00 to 17:00
    speed     5837 x 60 / 540 = 649 km/h
    649 is under 900  <- NO ALERT
