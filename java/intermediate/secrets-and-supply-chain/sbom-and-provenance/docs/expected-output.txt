THREE. Checking before deploying.
  THE GATE'S POLICY (what it trusts):
    key         shop-ci-2026 (an Ed25519 public key)
    builder     https://build.shop.example/runner/v1
    repository  https://git.shop.example/shop/store
    ref         refs/heads/main
  THE CHECKS, IN ORDER:
    1 provenance  2 signature  3 statement  4 subject
    5 builder  6 repository  7 branch
  EIGHT RELEASES OFFERED (the input):
    A  the release from the trusted builder, from main
    B  a build of a fork
    C  a build of an unreviewed branch
    D  a build signed on a developer's laptop
    E  the trusted key, naming the staging runner
    F  the fork's statement, edited to say main's repo
    G  the fork's jar with the release's provenance
    H  a jar with no provenance at all
  THE GATE'S ANSWERS (the result):
    A  deployed      all 7 checks pass
    B  refused at 6  built from another repository
    C  refused at 7  built from another branch
    D  refused at 2  no signature from a trusted builder
    E  refused at 5  built by a builder that is not trusted
    F  refused at 2  the signature does not match the statement
    G  refused at 4  the statement is about another artifact
    H  refused at 1  no provenance
  HOW TWO REFUSALS ARE MADE:
    F  the signed statement 772 bytes, names the fork
       the edited statement 768 bytes, names main's repo
       Ed25519 over the edited bytes: false
    G  the subject  sha256 38a6dd0dd912fc89...
       the jar      sha256 6d76d2c9bc23cc20...
