--- the bill
a scanner reports what a server offers; it does not say who can still read yesterday's traffic
a downgrade needs a network that fails and a client that retries: remove the retry or sign it with the SCSV
forward secrecy is a property of the key exchange, not of the key length
a clean scan today says nothing about traffic recorded under the old settings
