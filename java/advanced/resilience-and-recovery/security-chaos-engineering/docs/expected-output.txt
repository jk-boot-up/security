ONE. The service, the faults and the steady state.
  THE CONTROLS (each one a call to a dependency):
    control        dependency     first version, when it fails
    rate limit     ratelimit      no limit
    revocation     revocation     not revoked (soft fail)
    authorization  authorization  allow
    fraud score    fraud          a clean score
    audit trail    audit          drop the record
  THE FAULTS (Toxiproxy 2.13.0, one proxy per dependency):
    down        the proxy is disabled, connections are refused
    slow        every answer is held back 5000 ms
    hang        accepted, and then nothing is ever sent
    reset       the connection is reset by the peer
    trickle     the answer trickles at 1 KB per second
    slow close  the connection closes 3000 ms after the answer
  THE DEADLINE: 1500 ms for the whole call, body included
  THE STEADY STATE (before every experiment, both versions):
         amount  cert   decision
    s1      120  ok-1   approved: screened
    s2       80  ok-1   approved: screened
    s3     9000  ok-1   denied: fraud score
    s4      120  rev-7  denied: certificate
    audit records received: 4 of 4
    steady state held before 20 of 20 runs

THREE. The fraud score: a hang and a trickle.
  THE HYPOTHESES (2 experiments, one fault each):
    E4   no payment is approved without a fraud score
    E5   none is approved without a score when it trickles
  THE FIRST VERSION (no score is read as a clean score):
    E4   hang        unscreened 6 of 6, review 0  BROKEN
