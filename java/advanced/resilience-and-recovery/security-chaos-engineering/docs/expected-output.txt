--- the service under test
a bank payments service with 5 controls, each reached through its own Toxiproxy proxy: rate limit, certificate revocation, authorization, fraud score, audit trail
experiments: 10, each with 2 hypotheses (the control holds, the alert fires)
call deadline: bounded, failures injected: down, latency, hang, reset, trickle, slow close
=== first version
--- E1 authorization service down
hypothesis: no payment is approved while authorization cannot be reached
injected: authorization, the proxy is disabled, connections are refused
steady state before: yes
observed: approved 6 of 6
control: BROKEN, fails open
alert: no monitor, none raised -> BROKEN
--- E2 authorization service slow
hypothesis: no payment is approved when the authorization answer comes after the deadline
injected: authorization, every answer is held back 5000 ms
steady state before: yes
observed: approved 6 of 6
control: BROKEN, fails open
alert: no monitor, none raised -> BROKEN
--- E3 authorization connection reset
hypothesis: no payment is approved when the authorization connection is reset
injected: authorization, the connection is reset by the peer
steady state before: yes
observed: approved 6 of 6
control: BROKEN, fails open
alert: no monitor, none raised -> BROKEN
--- E4 fraud service hangs
hypothesis: no payment is approved without a fraud score
injected: fraud, the connection is accepted and then nothing is ever sent
steady state before: yes
observed: approved without a fraud score 6 of 6, held for review 0
control: BROKEN, payments go unscreened
alert: no monitor, none raised -> BROKEN
--- E5 fraud service on a trickle
hypothesis: no payment is approved without a fraud score when the answer arrives too slowly
injected: fraud, the answer trickles at 1 KB per second
steady state before: yes
observed: approved without a fraud score 6 of 6, held for review 0
control: BROKEN, payments go unscreened
alert: no monitor, none raised -> BROKEN
--- E6 audit sink down during a burst
hypothesis: every decision is in the audit sink once the sink is back
injected: audit, the proxy is disabled, connections are refused
steady state before: yes
observed: payments approved 20 of 20, audit records lost 20 of 20
control: BROKEN, audit records silently lost
alert: no monitor, none raised -> BROKEN
--- E7 audit sink closes slowly
hypothesis: a slow close loses no audit record and raises no false alarm
injected: audit, the connection closes 3000 ms after the answer
steady state before: yes
observed: payments approved 4 of 4, audit records lost 0 of 4
control: held
alert: no monitor, none raised -> held
--- E8 rate-limit store resets connections
hypothesis: one account never gets more than 5 payments approved in a burst
injected: ratelimit, the connection is reset by the peer
steady state before: yes
observed: approved 40 of 40 from one account, limit 5
control: BROKEN, the limit disappears
alert: no monitor, none raised -> BROKEN
--- E9 revocation responder down
hypothesis: a payment signed with a revoked certificate is never approved
injected: revocation, the proxy is disabled, connections are refused
steady state before: yes
observed: approved with a revoked certificate 6 of 6
control: BROKEN, the check soft-fails
alert: no monitor, none raised -> BROKEN
--- E10 revocation responder slow
hypothesis: a payment signed with a revoked certificate is never approved when the answer is late
injected: revocation, every answer is held back 5000 ms
steady state before: yes
observed: approved with a revoked certificate 6 of 6
control: BROKEN, the check soft-fails
alert: no monitor, none raised -> BROKEN
scoreboard, first version: held 2 of 20, broken 18

--- what the hardened version still accepts
certificate revoked while the responder is down, last answer was good: approved 1 of 1
--- the bill
a control that cannot be reached must decide: the first version decided yes on authorization, fraud, rate limit and revocation
an audit buffer that drops is a control that fails silently: the lost records are only found by counting them
an alert is a control too: without probes, 9 of the 9 outages that needed one were never reported
fail closed has a price: payments wait in review or are refused while the dependency is down
