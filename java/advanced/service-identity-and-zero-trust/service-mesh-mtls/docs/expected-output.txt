--- the bill
the mesh gives each pod a name taken from its ServiceAccount and encrypts between proxies: it does not decide who may call until a policy says so
a policy covers the ports that have a Server and the callers that go through a proxy
the mesh judges the caller, never the request: an allowed caller can send anything
the identity is only as good as who may create pods with a ServiceAccount
a certificate is trusted until it expires: the lifetime is how long a stolen one is useful
whoever has root on a node can read what the proxy hands to the app
