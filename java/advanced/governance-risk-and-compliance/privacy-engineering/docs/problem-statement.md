# Problem Statement

## The scenario

A bank wants to collect less, keep it for less time, share it more safely, and answer a customer who asks for their data to be erased.

## The naive version

Remove the name column from the extract and delete one row on request.

## What this project must deliver

- A PostgreSQL database of customers, accounts, transactions and notes.
- Minimization against purposes and a measure of who can be picked out.
- Keyed pseudonyms and a retention job with a legal hold.
- An export and an erasure request with a scan for what remains.
