# Session Guide — Risk Management

## Learning Objectives

By the end of the session you can:

- turn a three-point estimate into a distribution
- explain why a heat map ranking differs from an expected-loss ranking
- choose controls within a budget by net benefit
- check residual risk against an appetite

## Walkthrough

Run the demo and compare the two top-three lists. In the fourth act find the budget that would bring the bank inside its appetite.

## Exercises

1. Add a risk of your own with ranges and see where it ranks.
2. Add correlation: let ransomware and card breach happen in the same years.
3. Change the appetite to 10% and find the cheapest set that meets it.
4. Add insurance as a control that caps the loss of an event.
