# Problem Statement

## The scenario

A bank's risk committee wants to know what its controls budget buys.

## The naive version

Rank the risks on a heat map and buy what looks red.

## What this project must deliver

- A register with three-point estimates.
- A seeded Monte Carlo simulation of 50,000 years.
- The best controls within a budget, found by trying every set.
- Residual risk checked against an appetite.
