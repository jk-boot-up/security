# Session Guide — Threats and Risk

## Learning Objectives

By the end of the session you can:

- define asset, threat, vulnerability, risk and attack surface in your own words
- score a risk as likelihood times impact and rate it low, medium or high
- rank a list of risks and explain how a tie is broken
- explain why a risk score is a judgment, and what should be written down because of it

## Walkthrough

Start with the house-owner analogy and ask the group to count the entry points of
their own home. Then run the demo one act at a time. In act two, ask the group to
name the asset, the threat and the vulnerability before reading them out. In act
four, ask which treatment they would fund first before the planner shows its
order, and discuss any difference. In act five, ask the group to score one risk
on their own, and compare the numbers.

## Exercises

1. Add a new entry point, POST /coupons, and a risk for it. Where does it land in the ranking?
2. Change the budget to 5. Which treatments are funded, and what is the worst risk left?
3. Change the rating bands so that 10 and above is high. Which tests must change?
4. Score the register as a third reviewer, the shop's owner, and compare the top three with engineering and finance.
